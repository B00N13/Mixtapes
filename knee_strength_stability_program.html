1. Ankle Plantar Flexion / Dorsi Flexion — Wobble Board

Sets3
Duration1 min
FocusAnkle control

How to do it

  1. Stand on a wobble board with your feet hip-width apart.
  2. Keep your knees and hips straight as you seesaw the board forwards and backwards.
  3. Keep the movement smooth.

2. Isometric Wall Sit

Sets3
Reps6
Hold90 sec

How to do it

  1. Stand upright with a wall behind you.
  2. Lean your back and buttocks against the wall, then walk your feet forward.
  3. Slide down until your hips and knees reach approximately 90 degrees.
  4. Keep your back and buttocks in contact with the wall.
  5. Hold the position for the prescribed time.

3. Eccentric Deep Single-Leg Squat

Sets3
Reps10
FocusEccentric control

How to do it

  1. Stand upright with a chair behind you and take a small step away from it.
  2. Transfer your weight onto the leg you want to exercise and lift the other leg slightly in front.
  3. Look straight ahead, brace your abdominal muscles and keep your back straight.
  4. Bend the working knee and push your hips back toward the chair.
  5. Keep the knee pointing directly ahead and do not allow it to drop inward.
  6. Once seated, lower the raised leg to the floor and stand using both legs.
  7. Repeat.

4. Split Squat — Rear Foot Elevated (Bulgarian)

Sets3
Reps10
Additional instruction8–10 with support

How to do it

  1. Stand with your back to the step and place your rear leg onto the step.
  2. Create a large distance between your front and back foot.
  3. Use a stick behind your shoulders for balance if needed.
  4. Bend the front knee and drop your hips straight down toward the ground.
  5. Straighten back up.
  6. Keep the front knee from moving in front of the front toe and keep the hips moving straight down.
Source wording: the PhysiApp sheet also says “8–10 with support” as an additional instruction while the populated prescription shows 3 sets × 10 reps.

5. Resisted Hip Abduction / External Rotation — Crab Walks

Sets3
RepsNot specified
InstructionTo fatigue

How to do it

  1. Stand upright with your feet together.
  2. Use resistance bands above and below the knees as prescribed.
  3. Engage your deep abdominal muscles throughout.
  4. Keep your spine neutral and your knees over your ankles while hinging at the hips.
  5. Push your knees apart to create tension in the band and form a diamond shape with your legs.
  6. Maintain that position while taking small steps to one side, then the other.
  7. Repeat to fatigue.
